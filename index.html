Frequently asked

How much does Madfun charge?

A 4% platform fee is deducted from every ticket sold. Any other applicable transaction or service costs are communicated separately.

Can I withdraw before event day?

Verified organizers may request up to 50% of eligible available earnings, subject to the applicable checks and conditions.

Can my team validate tickets?

Yes. Use your own team with the Madfun Validator App or request trained Madfun validators at an additional cost.

Can Madfun plan the whole event?

Yes. Support can cover planning, production, venue operations and guest entry—or only the areas where you need help.
